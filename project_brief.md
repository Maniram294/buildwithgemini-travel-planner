# My agent: travel-planner
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps travelers discover destinations and plan custom itineraries with a catalog of curated travel experiences, stays, and activities.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers travel style (budget, luxury, adventure), dietary needs, home airport, and previous destinations visited
- Tools: Look up destination details, check local weather, retrieve attractions and lodging, and bookmark stops
- Catalog/UI: Curated destinations, hotels, and activity lists that render as rich A2UI cards and day-by-day itinerary tables
- Image gen: Generates scenic destination postcards and visual moodboards
- Sandbox: Calculates budget breakdowns, currency conversions, and travel cost estimates

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for budget/currency calculations, live Google Maps / Places API, Omni video previews
